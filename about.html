Her approach

A plan made with you, not just for you.

Dr. Grayson Cline founded Abundant Health Chiropractic after returning to her hometown of Stoughton, Wisconsin. Her stated goal is to work with patients to create a plan that helps them feel and move better, while giving them practical tools to support their progress beyond the appointment.

Her practitioner biography emphasizes a collaborative approach: understanding what is going on, choosing techniques that suit the person and visit, and supporting patients with useful strategies and movement-based care where appropriate.

“My goal is to create a plan with the patient that gets them feeling and moving better in the shortest amount of time and give you all the tools to reduce the chances of things happening again.”

Techniques and care approaches

Dr. Cline’s bio lists Diversified, Gonstead, Thompson, and Activator adjusting techniques; extremity adjusting; instrument-assisted soft-tissue manipulation; myofascial release, cupping, and taping; rehabilitation; and balance training.

The technique used depends on the individual and the appointment. The best way to understand what may be appropriate for your needs is to discuss it with Dr. Cline directly.
