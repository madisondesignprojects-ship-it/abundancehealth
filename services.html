Chiropractic adjustments

Soft-tissue modalities

The practice describes soft-tissue approaches including myofascial release, trigger-point techniques, and active-release techniques. Dr. Cline’s booking-page bio also mentions instrument-assisted soft-tissue manipulation and taping. Ask which options are currently available and whether they carry a separate charge.

Cupping therapy

Cupping uses cups placed on the skin to create suction and may be used as a soft-tissue modality. Whether it is appropriate and whether it is a standalone or add-on service should be confirmed with the practice.

Price not separately listed Confirm with the clinic

Infrared sauna

The practice lists infrared sauna as a separate wellness option. JaneApp shows a 40-minute appointment but does not currently show a rate. The official practice service page lists $30 for one session, $125 for a 5-pack, and $225 for a 10-pack.

Intersegmental traction table

Red light therapy
