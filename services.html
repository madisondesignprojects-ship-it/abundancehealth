OTHER SERVICES

Additional therapies and techniques

The practice lists the following services. Separate booking options and prices are not available for every service, so contact the clinic to confirm what is currently offered.

01

Chiropractic adjustments

Dr. Cline's practitioner bio lists Diversified, Gonstead, Thompson, Activator, and extremity adjusting. The appropriate technique depends on the patient and the appointment.

02

Soft-tissue modalities and cupping

The practice lists soft-tissue modalities, myofascial release, cupping, and taping among its care approaches. Ask which options fit your visit and what fees apply.

03

Intersegmental traction table

A table-based modality using moving rollers to provide rhythmic movement along the back. Ask the clinic whether it is available as part of your care.

04

Red light therapy

Red light therapy is listed on the practice's current services page. Contact the clinic for current availability, appointment length, and fees.

05

Rehabilitation and balance training

Dr. Cline's bio lists rehabilitation and balance training among her approaches. Ask whether movement or exercise-based options are appropriate for your goals.

06

Nutrition education

Dr. Cline completed a nutrition certification in 2024. Contact the clinic to understand what nutrition-related services are currently offered.
