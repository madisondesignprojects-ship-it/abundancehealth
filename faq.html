Frequently asked

Answers before you book.

If your question is not covered here, contact the clinic directly. We'll keep the information here grounded in the practice's published details.

What happens during the free consultation?

JaneApp lists a free consultation with Dr. Grayson Cline for 15 minutes at $0. It is a starting point for a conversation and questions. Confirm any additional details directly with the clinic; the consultation listing does not imply that treatment is included.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the Book an Appointment button to open the clinic’s existing JaneApp booking page. The current listed options include New Patient Chiropractic (40 minutes, $120), Chiropractic Adjustment (20 minutes, $60), Free Consultation (15 minutes, $0), and Infrared Sauna (40 minutes; a price is not shown in JaneApp).

What services does the practice offer?

The practice website lists chiropractic adjustments, cupping therapy, infrared sauna, intersegmental traction, soft-tissue modalities, and red light therapy. Dr. Cline’s bio also mentions several adjustment approaches, extremity adjusting, rehabilitation, balance training, and nutrition certification. Ask the practice about current availability and whether each modality is a standalone appointment.

How much does a visit cost?

The current JaneApp menu lists a new patient chiropractic appointment at $120 for 40 minutes, a chiropractic adjustment at $60 for 20 minutes, and a 15-minute free consultation at $0. The practice website lists infrared sauna pricing of $30 for one session, $125 for a 5-pack, and $225 for a 10-pack, but JaneApp does not show a sauna price. Confirm all rates with the clinic before booking.

Does the practice accept insurance?

The practice’s existing FAQ describes Abundant Health Chiropractic as an out-of-network, fee-for-service provider and says it accepts most HSA and FSA arrangements. Check directly with the practice about current payment procedures and your specific circumstances.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

The booking menu does not specify a complete list of items to bring. Contact the clinic before your appointment if you need to know whether there are forms, records, or other information you should prepare.

Where is the practice located?

Abundant Health Chiropractic is listed at 318 S Water Street, Stoughton, WI 53589. Use the directions link on the Contact page. Clinic hours are displayed there as published on the practice website; appointment availability is managed through JaneApp.

Can I buy a gift card?

Yes. The existing JaneApp site links to an online gift-card page. Use the Gift Cards link in the footer to open it.

Is nutritional counseling available as a separate appointment?

The existing practice website mentions nutritional counseling, and Dr. Cline’s bio notes a nutrition certification completed in 2024. The current JaneApp appointment menu does not list nutritional counseling as a separate booking category, so please contact the clinic to confirm availability and pricing.

Do you offer massage therapy?

Massage therapy is mentioned on the existing website FAQ, but it does not appear as a separate category in the current JaneApp booking menu. Contact the clinic to confirm whether it is currently offered and how it is scheduled.
