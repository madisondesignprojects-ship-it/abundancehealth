What services are available?

The practice website lists chiropractic adjustments, cupping, infrared sauna, intersegmental traction, soft-tissue modalities, and red light therapy. Dr. Cline's biography also lists rehabilitation and balance training. Confirm current availability and separate pricing with the clinic.
